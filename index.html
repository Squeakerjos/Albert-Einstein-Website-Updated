About Me

Hello, I'm Albert Einstein! I was born in Germany in 1879 and became a theoretical physicist.

I am best known for developing the theory of relativity. My work helped people understand space, time, light, and energy.

I received the 1921 Nobel Prize in Physics for my explanation of the photoelectric effect.
